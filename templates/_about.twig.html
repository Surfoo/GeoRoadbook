Privacy policy

GeoRoadbook collects some information about the connected user in order to keep them connected to this application with a session cookie.
GeoRoadbook collects your Pocket Queries if you are connected and a premium member.
GeoRoadbook doesn't share any of your personal data with a third party.
Your created roadbooks are deleted after 30 days without any modification; you can exercise your data rights by sending the URL of the roadbook to delete by email (see below).
